Frequently Asked Questions

▶ How do I place an order?

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, and enter your delivery details. Choose a payment method and confirm your order. You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking details.

▶ What are the delivery fees?

Delivery fees are typically $2.99, but may vary based on your location and order size. Free delivery is available for orders above $50 in selected areas.

▶ How long does delivery take?

Our standard delivery time is 30 minutes. During peak hours, it may take up to 45 minutes. You can track your order in real-time through your dashboard.

▶ Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it. After that, the order will be prepared by the restaurant. For cancellations, contact our support team immediately.

▶ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, debit cards, PayPal, digital wallets, and cash on delivery. All card transactions are secured with SSL encryption.
